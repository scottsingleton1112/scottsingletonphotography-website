Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

Scott Singleton Photography ("I", "me") is run by Scott Singleton, based in Huddersfield, West Yorkshire. This policy explains what personal information I collect and how I use it.

What I collect

When you use the enquiry form or email me, I collect the details you send: your names, email address, wedding date, venue and anything else you choose to tell me. If you book, I'll also hold the details needed for your contract and payments.

How I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, plan and photograph your wedding, and manage your booking. I don't sell your information or add you to marketing lists without asking.

Who else sees it

Enquiry form messages are delivered to my email by FormSubmit, and stored in Google (Gmail). Your photos are delivered through an online gallery service. These providers only process data to provide their service.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Booking records are kept for as long as needed for tax and legal reasons, usually 6 years.

Your photos

I may share a selection of photos from your wedding on my website and social media. If you'd rather I didn't, just tell me and I'll respect that.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time by emailing scottsingletonphotography@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.
