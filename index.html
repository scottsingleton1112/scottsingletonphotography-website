Questions

The things couples usually ask

Do you travel?

Yes. Anywhere within 30 miles of Huddersfield is included, and further afield is 45p a mile.

Will we still get group photos?

Yes. Send me your list before the day and I'll work through it quickly, usually in 20 minutes or less, so you can get back to your guests.

How long until we get our photos?

A sneak peek within 7 days and your full gallery within 8 weeks, often sooner.

We're awkward in front of a camera. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most couples say this. The day is mostly candid, and for portraits I'll give you simple things to do rather than poses to hold.

Do you offer video?

No, I focus on photography only. I'm happy to work alongside your videographer.

What happens if you're ill on the day?

It's very unlikely, but if I can't make it I'll arrange a suitable photographer to cover your wedding. If that isn't possible, you'll get a full refund.

What kit do you use?

Professional Canon mirrorless cameras with fast lenses, so dark churches and evening receptions are no problem.

Can we have an unplugged ceremony?

Yes, and I'd recommend it. Your officiant can ask guests to put phones away, so you get photos of faces rather than screens.
